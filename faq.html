Private Real Estate Financing FAQ

What is private real estate lending?

Private lending involves borrowing money from private investors or non-bank alternative funds rather than traditional institutions like commercial banks. These loans are primarily asset-backed, meaning the underwriting focuses heavily on the value of the real estate collateral rather than just the borrower's personal credit history.

How fast can I receive a Letter of Intent (LOI)?

Because private lenders bypass bureaucratic committee approvals, the initial review process is highly streamlined. You can typically expect a formal Letter of Intent (LOI) within three (3) business days of submitting your initial project summary and loan request.

What is the typical timeline to close and deploy funds?

Once the LOI is signed and terms are agreed upon, the underwriting and title verification move quickly. Capital can generally be fully deployed, and the loan closed, within two (2) weeks. This speed makes private financing an ideal choice for time-sensitive acquisitions or construction timelines.

What property types qualify for private financing?

Our private sources typically fund non-owner-occupied properties. This includes commercial assets, multi-family residential buildings, ground-up construction projects, major renovations, or fix-and-flip properties.

What documentation is required to get started?

To initiate the process, you will generally need an executive summary detailing the loan request, a breakdown of project costs, a clear exit strategy, and basic financial statements. While items like property appraisals and title reports are required before closing, they are not always mandatory to get the initial discussion started.
